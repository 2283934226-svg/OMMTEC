01 / About us 关于我们

A small, hands-on team with a serious appetite for robotics.

Based in Room A106, First Discipline Building, Fengxian Campus, Shanghai Institute of Technology, we explore modern manufacturing through real robots, real competitions, and intelligent control.

Our work bridges mechanical design, embedded systems, perception, and machine learning—from the intensity of RoboMaster to the open frontier of reinforcement learning.
