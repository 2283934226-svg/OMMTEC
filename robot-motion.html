Robot Motion 机器人的运动

OMMTEC / Research direction 05

From coordinated joints to movement in the real world.

Robot motion brings joint coordination, balance, and interaction with the surroundings into a physical experiment. These short recordings document humanoid stepping trials in our laboratory, with the team observing the robot beside a set of steps and a support frame.

从关节协调到实机运动：通过实验室中的迈步测试，观察人形机器人的动作与姿态变化。

01 / Motion in practice

Observing each step

Two views of the testing process, presented together for a closer look at leg movement, body posture, and the experimental setup.

Videos loop silently while in view. Use the lower-right sound button to listen.

02 / What we look for

Coordination, posture, and repeatability

Physical trials help us inspect how individual joint movements combine into a complete action. Foot placement, body alignment, and repeatable movement are useful points of observation as we develop our robot motion work.
